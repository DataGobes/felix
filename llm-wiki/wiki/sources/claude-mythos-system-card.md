## Significance

This is the longest and most detailed system card Anthropic has published. It documents a model that was **not released publicly** due to safety concerns--instead deployed only for defensive cybersecurity via "Project Glasswing." The card introduces several new assessment methods including white-box interpretability of alignment-relevant activations, emotion probes, and open-ended self-interactions. [Felix:chunk:7]
